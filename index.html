About us

Form Helper is a small project built by one developer, Ankush. It is made to save time and keep your everyday details in your own hands.

Who made this?

Form Helper is built and maintained by a single developer, Ankush. There is no company or investor behind it.

Why does it exist?

Students and people using shared computers often type the same details again and again. This tool makes that faster and keeps the data on the device.

Do you have access to my data?

No. We never receive, store, or see the details you save. They stay in your browser only.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

Only in your browser's local storage, on the device you use.

Can it save Aadhaar or bank details?

No. It is designed for everyday contact and address details only.

How do I remove my data on a shared computer?

Press Erase all before you leave.

Does it cost money?

No. It is free to use.

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

We do not collect your data

We never take, collect, store, or sell your data. The details you type into Form Helper are saved only in your own browser, using local storage on your device.

No servers, no accounts

Form Helper does not have a server that receives your information, and there are no accounts or sign-ups.

Your control

You can erase all saved details at any time with the Erase all button. Clearing your browser data also removes them.

Shared computers

If you use a shared computer, please press Erase all before you leave.
